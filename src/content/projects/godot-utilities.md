## GodotCodes

A small library of things I was tired of rewriting.

- A **platformer controller** with coyote time, jump buffering and variable jump height.
- **Diagonal and circular containers**: custom layout nodes for menus that are not plain lists. Both are in Phase Reveal, and the diagonal one is in Situs Tertinggal.
- A title-screen template with a signal hub.

## GodotGamLAB

Material for the sessions I run as head of the game development division: eight GDScript lessons from variables to functions, then a space shooter built step by step.
